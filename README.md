# Buyer Outreach Dashboard

**Live Demo:** https://poetic-parfait-b0ad88.netlify.app/

---

## Overview

The Buyer Outreach Dashboard helps sales and business development teams manage buyer outreach efficiently. It allows users to add leads, organize buyer information, generate professional email drafts, track communication status, and export lead data.



The goal is **not just to manage leads**, but to help you:

- Find genuine buyers
- Store buyer information
- Generate professional outreach emails
- Track communication
- Follow up with interested buyers
- Convert leads into customers

> **Note:** This internship is performance-based. Your success depends on the quality of buyers you find and the conversions you achieve—not on how many leads you add.

# Dashboard Features

## Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides a quick overview of your outreach activities.

It displays:

- Total Leads
- New Leads
- Contacted
- Replied
- Converted

This helps monitor overall progress.

---

## Product & Sender Settings

Before contacting buyers, fill in your company details.

Required fields:

- Your Name
- Company Name
- Product Name
- Product USP (Unique Selling Proposition)
- Call To Action

Example

| Field | Example |
|--------|---------|
| Name | John Smith |
| Company | Sunrise Exports Pvt. Ltd. |
| Product | Organic Cotton Fabric |
| USP | Premium Quality • Competitive Pricing |
| CTA | Schedule a short meeting this week |

These details are automatically used while generating emails.

# Finding Buyers

Search for companies interested in importing your products.

Recommended sources:

- IndiaMART
- TradeIndia
- Alibaba
- Company Websites
- Export Directories
- Industry Associations
- Referrals

Avoid automated scraping from platforms like LinkedIn or Google unless you have permission to do so.

# Generate Email

Click

```
Draft
```

The system automatically creates a personalized outreach email.

Example

```text
Subject: Premium Organic Cotton Fabric from India

Hi David,

I hope you're doing well.

I'm John Smith from Sunrise Exports Pvt. Ltd.

We manufacture premium organic cotton fabric for international buyers.

Our products are known for their quality, competitive pricing, and reliable delivery.

Would you be available for a short meeting this week?
I'd be happy to share our catalogue and pricing.

Looking forward to hearing from you.

Best Regards,

John Smith
Sunrise Exports Pvt. Ltd.
```

Edit the email if needed before sending.

---

# Send Email

After reviewing the draft:

1. Copy the email
2. Paste it into Gmail or Outlook
3. Send it manually

If your dashboard supports email integration in the future, this process can be automated.

# Follow-up

Not every buyer replies immediately.

Send a polite follow-up after several days if there is no response.

Good follow-ups often increase reply rates.

# Best Practices

- Research companies before contacting them.
- Personalize every email.
- Keep emails short and professional.
- Track every interaction.
- Follow up politely.
- Update lead statuses regularly.
- Focus on quality leads rather than quantity.

- Finding relevant buyers

- Maintaining organized lead records
